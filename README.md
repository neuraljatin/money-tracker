# Money Tracker

A simple spending and savings tracker for iPhone. Add it to your Home Screen from Safari and it works offline.

- Log expenses, income and savings
- Confirm a payment from an iPhone Shortcut or a bank SMS
- See monthly totals, charts and budgets
- Download an Excel sheet of your transactions and summaries
- Keep a JSON backup on your phone

All data stays on your device. Shortcut details go after `#` in the address, so they never reach a server.

Live app: https://neuraljatin.github.io/money-tracker/

You can also open this folder in a browser. Setup steps for the iPhone Shortcut are in SHORTCUT.md.

**Do this first:** add the app to your Home Screen. iPhone can clear a normal Safari site’s saved data after about a week without a visit. A Home Screen app is mostly kept.
