    About & info

    Money Tracker
    Logs every rupee. A bank SMS or Apple Pay payment asks “Log this spend?”, and one tap saves it. Everything stays on your phone.

    Features

    • Auto-detect spends from bank SMS (Axis, OneCard, UPI) and Apple Pay on Axis cards
    • Confirm screen filled in with amount, merchant, method and a guessed category. Save or Skip.
    • Home: month picker, income, spent, savings, net and savings rate
    • Charts by category, by day, 6-month income vs expenses, and by payment method
    • Budgets per category with green, amber and red bars
    • Activity: search, filter, edit, swipe to delete (with Undo), Needs review list
    • Excel export with 4 sheets, JSON backup and restore, CSV import
    • Works offline, dark and light mode, Glass effect switch

    How auto-detect works

    1. Add to Home Screen first. iPhone keeps a Home Screen app’s data but may clear a website’s data.
    2. Shortcuts → Automation → Message: when a text contains “debited”, the Shortcut reads the amount and merchant.
    3. Shortcuts → Automation → Wallet: runs when you pay with Apple Pay on your Axis cards.
    4. The app opens at #confirm&amount=…. The details sit after #, so they never leave the phone.
    5. Check the category and tap Save. Skip moves it to Needs review.

    “Credited” means income. The same amount within 10 minutes shows a duplicate warning (a matching merchant makes it “very likely”), but you can still save it.

    • DoneAsk iPhone to keep the app’s storage (persistent storage)
    • DoneVersion shown in Settings, and new builds replace the old cache
    • DoneNeeds review list for SMS the app couldn’t read
    • DoneGlass effect switch, Reduce Transparency and Reduce Motion support
